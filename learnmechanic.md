## 💼 My Start and Route to Journeyman Mechanic 

I was always curious about mechanical things growing up. That curiosity got me ìn trouble at times. Like a lot of kids curious about things I would take stuff apart. In the early attempts they rarely worked when re-assembled. We once had a new lawnmower. It quit running likely due to bad gas. I tore into it no knowledge of pullers or anything else. I took bolts out and when a part wouldn't come off I beat on it until it came apart. In the process I ruined the flywheel and knocked a hole in crankcase. As I got older and by trial and error and reading magazines and manuals I decided on a career as an Automotive Mechanic. I started with family and friends cars and eventually worked at Firestone and Western Auto repair shops at 18. I had a voracious appetite for technical literature. this helped improve my automotive repair skills. I worked many jobs after my start. I literally worked at 50 positions by my mid 20's. I progressed to heavy truck repair after starting with Wales Transportation in 1981 as a heavy trailer mechanic.
