#The Beginng and Learning to Mechanic

I was always curious about mechanical things growing up. That curiosity got me ìn trouble at times. Like a lot of kids curious about things I would take stuff apart. In the early attempts they rarely worked worked when re-assembled. We once had a pretty new lawnmower. It quit running likely due to bad gas. I literally tore into. No knowledge of pullers or anything else. I took bolts out and when a part wouldn't come off I beat on it until came off. In the process ruined the flywheel and knocked a hole in crankcase. As I got older and by trial and error and reading magazines and manuals became an OK mechanic. I started with family cars and eventually worked at Firestone and Western Auto repair shops. I had a voracious appetite for technical literature. this helped improve my automotive repair skills. I worked many jobs after my start. literally worked at a hundred positions by my late 20's. I progressed to heavy trucks after starting with Wales Transportation in Grand Prairie.
